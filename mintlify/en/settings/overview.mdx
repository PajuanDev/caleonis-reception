## Settings pages

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Usage** | Current billing-period usage, remaining included usage, reset timing, and metered usage. |
| **Billing** | Current plan, upgrades, subscription management, and spending caps. |
| **Team** | Member invitations, roles, and workspace access. |
| **Notifications** | Email and SMS alerts for events that need attention. |
| **Phone number** | The active business number and eligible number replacement. |
| **Widget** | Widget keys, website chat, lead capture, browser calls, and installation. |
| **Account** | Account email and password changes. |
| **Appearance** | Dashboard language, time format, and theme. |

## Update account details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the dashboard sidebar, go to **Manage** > **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a settings page">
    Select the page for the workspace or account setting you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    Update the field or action you need. Some changes, such as email updates, require confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabled features

Pages depend on role, version and enabled capabilities. Appearance controls may set language, time format and theme; check the result after reload. Commercial plans remain subject to approval and new widget keys are restricted. Requalify number, calendar and booking changes before customer use.
