The **Integrations** page connects Caléonis Receptionist to Google Calendar and sends events to your own tools with webhooks. To connect a CRM or Zapier, use webhooks and the [Caléonis Receptionist API](/en/api/overview).

Each integration guide explains what the connection does, what access it needs, and how to confirm it is working.

## Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar when you want Caléonis Receptionist to help callers book appointments. After you authorize Google, choose the calendar that should receive bookings.

## Webhooks

Add a webhook when another tool should react to Caléonis Receptionist, for example to log each finished call in your CRM.

## Where to find it

Open **Manage** > **Integrations** from the dashboard sidebar.

## Availability

The pilot qualified one Google booking on a test calendar; durable authorization needs a separate check. Qualify your specific webhook/API integration. The French Voice-only line provides no SMS, and a provider adapter does not establish delivery.
