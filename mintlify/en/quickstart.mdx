---
title: "Prepare your first test"
description: "Set up a safe pilot scenario before forwarding customer calls."
---
Use fictional details, your own contact information and a test calendar. A telephone call may incur provider charges.

## Set up

1. Open [Caléonis Receptionist](https://receptionist.caleonis.com) and verify the active business.
2. Review **Agent > AI settings** and write your business greeting.
3. Add accurate opening hours, location, service area and answers under **Knowledge**.
4. Review **Services** and **Rules**.
5. Start a new browser test call when that control is available and allow the microphone.
6. Review the call record, transcript and summary after ending the call.

Configuration changes apply to new conversations; an active call may keep its original context.

## Test booking separately

Connect Google Calendar, select a writable test calendar and verify the business time zone. Check the booking mode and service duration.

Make an authorised fictional booking, then read it in both the dashboard and Google Calendar. A pending request or sync is not an externally confirmed booking.

A booking may exist without an email, SMS or invited guest. Respect a refusal of notifications.

## Before telephone use

Verify that the number belongs to the correct business and is ready for inbound calls. The French pilot number is Voice-only, so do not promise SMS.

Test the full scenario before customer forwarding. Keep a human fallback and a way to disable forwarding with your operator.

Human transfer and durable Google OAuth renewal need explicit qualification. Do not test writes, calls or messages involving a third party without authorisation.
