Caléonis Receptionist is one AI receptionist for your business. It uses your greeting, knowledge, services and rules, with calls and activity in the [dashboard](https://receptionist.caleonis.com).

The complete help centre is currently French first. Start with the [French setup guide](/quickstart) or the [English quickstart](/en/quickstart).

## Pilot availability

The pilot qualified inbound telephone conversations, private call review and a Google Calendar booking in a test configuration. A new customer workspace needs its own checks.

The French pilot number is **Voice-only** and cannot send or receive SMS. The Messages page may therefore be empty. A callback request does not trigger an automatic outbound call.

Google OAuth renewal needs a separate durability check. Human transfer, cancellation and rescheduling must not be described as qualified without their own end-to-end evidence.

New website widget keys are restricted by default. Existing authorised configurations are separate from browser voice testing.

## Commercial and medical limits

Prices, plans and quotas are on request until approved. Legacy LobbyStack plan names do not define a Caléonis offer.

Medical practices must limit the pilot to administrative reception without health data. No HDS qualification or medical guarantee is claimed.
