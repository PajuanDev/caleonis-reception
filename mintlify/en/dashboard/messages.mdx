The **Messages** page combines SMS and website chat for the active business. Operators can review inbound and outbound messages, search conversations, send replies, and control AI handoff for website chat.

The AI doesn't reply to SMS. Texts sent to your business number wait here for a manual reply. Website chat is restricted and only appears for workspaces that already have widget keys.


## Find a conversation

The left panel shows conversations and their latest activity. Filter the list by **All**, **Web**, or **SMS**.

Each item shows:

- The contact, visitor, phone number, or email address.
- The latest message preview.
- The latest activity time.
- A web chat marker when the conversation came from the widget.

Use search to find conversations by contact details or the latest message.

## Review a conversation

Open a conversation to see its message timeline. The thread shows inbound customer messages, outbound AI or operator messages, and the time for each message.

## Send a reply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Select an SMS or website chat conversation from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your reply">
    Use the composer at the bottom of the thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Take over or resume AI

Website chat threads show their current automation state in the header. Click **Take over** when an operator should handle the conversation. Click **Resume AI** when the website assistant should respond again.

SMS threads do not show the AI handoff control. Replies from this page are manual.

## French Voice-only line

The pilot number cannot send or receive SMS. Messages remains accessible and may be empty. Manual replies require an active supported channel and permission; respect unavailable and disabled states. A callback request from a telephone call belongs to call detail and dashboard follow-up, not automatically an SMS thread. New widget issuance is off by default.
